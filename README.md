**Kalathil Properties** is the premier real estate, furnished short/long-term stay, and property management division of **Kalathil Group**, based in Kozhikode (Calicut), Kerala. We specialize in transparent, trust-driven real estate transactions and dependable family accommodations.

---

## 🧭 Core Divisions & Services

*   **Kalathil Realty (Buy & Sell):** Professional brokerage and guidance for residential houses, villas, luxury apartments, and investment plots across Kozhikode with all terms established in writing.
*   **Kalathil Stays (Rent):** A curated portfolio of fully furnished studio, 2BHK, and 3BHK homes starting from **₹10,000/month**, ideal for families, corporate relocations, and medical caregivers.
*   **Kalathil Management (NRI & Local Care):** End-to-end management for rental properties, handling tenant screening, routine inspections, maintenance, and collection reporting for local and overseas owners.
*   **Kalathil Developments:** Future-focused residential projects published only upon complete statutory approval.
